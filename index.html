Every formula begins with a feeling, not an ingredient. Our noses spend months in pursuit of a single chord — that precise moment when chemistry becomes emotion. Nothing leaves the atelier until it has earned its name.

The Atelier Process

I

Raw Material Selection

Only the rarest naturals. Bulgarian rose harvested at dawn. Oud from 60-year trees. Each ingredient tasted, smelled, and argued over for months.

II

Olfactive Composition

Our master perfumer works alone, in silence, over 18 months. No brief. No deadline. Only the pursuit of something true.

III

Maceration & Rest

Each formula macerates for a minimum of six weeks. Time is the final ingredient. There are no shortcuts in this atelier.

IV

Hand Bottling

Filled by hand in our Paris studio. Each flacon inspected, sealed with black wax, and numbered by our craftspeople.

BESPOKE

Your scent.
Yours alone.

The Ombré Bespoke programme is reserved for a small number of clients each year. Over four private sessions in our Paris atelier, our founder will compose a fragrance that belongs to no one else on earth.

It will never be replicated, never be sold. Only one bottle will exist.

An intimate one-on-one session in our historic Paris atelier. You'll meet with our master perfumer to discuss your memories, preferences, and the emotional journey you want your fragrance to capture.
Over four separate sessions spanning several months, you'll work side-by-side with our perfumer. Each meeting refines the composition—adjusting notes, balancing accords, and ensuring every nuance reflects your vision.
Your final composition is hand-bottled in a single Baccarat crystal flacon, engraved with your name and the date. The formula is destroyed. No second bottle will ever exist.
You receive a hand-lettered certificate detailing the composition's notes, the date of creation, and a wax seal confirming this fragrance exists nowhere else in the world.
